Cross-tabulation

Is this category associated with that one? A two-way table of counts with its row and column totals and percentages, beside stacked bars of the same numbers, and R's chi-square or Fisher's exact test of it, with R's warning when an expected count is too small for chi-square. Either variable is a column or a biomarker cut at its median, tertiles, quartiles or typed points. Click a count to list its participants and open a participant's profile. The data is the synthetic study: 200 made-up participants, and no real one.
