# association-scatter requirements matrix

> Requirement matrix for the bio.viz **association scatter** (`src/association-scatter.js` and `src/association-scatter/`, exported as `BioViz.associationScatter`). Written with task [bio.viz#26](https://github.com/jwildfire/bio.viz/issues/26), parent requirement [obot.roadmap#357](https://github.com/jwildfire/obot.roadmap/issues/357); design [353_design.html](https://jwildfire.github.io/obot.roadmap/requirements/design/353_design.html), sections Core chart types (association scatter), The shared core, How a p-value is shown, and Two places the line needs care. The chart's interface is written out in [`docs/association-scatter.md`](../docs/association-scatter.md).

## Scope

bio.viz's second chart. Two variables against one another, one point per participant: either axis a biomarker at a visit with a value type, or a participant-level number; points coloured by a group; panels by one further variable; a linear or logarithmic scale per axis; controls for all of them; filters when there is participant data; a region dragged across the points that lists its participants; safety.viz's participant profile from a point or a row of the list; a statistics line that prints what R answers; and a fitted line.

The chart is built from safety.viz's kit, loaded beside bio.viz on the page and never bundled into it, its rows come from the core's frame, and what it shares with the group comparison chart is written once, in `src/shared/`.

The statistics line prints a correlation coefficient that R computed: the chart chooses Pearson's or Spearman's, hands R the rows of each panel through the connection, and prints what gsm.bio's `Analyze_Correlation` returns, for everyone drawn and, with a colour, within each of its levels. The fitted line is R's too: gsm.bio's `Analyze_Fit` returns a linear fit or a smooth as points with their band, and the chart joins the points. The one line the chart draws by itself is y = x. In the browser, R is given gsm.bio's one statistics file, vendored here with a record of its commit, and what it answers on the gallery's demo is held to what desktop R answers for the same rows.

On a logarithmic axis R is handed the base-10 logarithm of the value, so what it computes is of the values as plotted, and the chart says so on the line.

Out of scope, and absent on purpose: the computation of any coefficient, interval, p-value, adjustment, fitted line, smooth or band. Those are R's. The chart does not replace safety.viz's shift plot or its delta-delta. The grid that opens this chart for a pair is the correlation matrix ([bio.viz#27](https://github.com/jwildfire/bio.viz/issues/27)), and the chart from R, with its statistics stored in the page, is gsm.bio's widget ([gsm.bio#13](https://github.com/jwildfire/gsm.bio/issues/13)).

## Requirement IDs

Prefix **AS**, with these areas: **KIT** (safety.viz's kit, the bundles, the shared parts and the absence of inference), **CFG** (settings), **DATA** (the points, the axes, the identity line and a region), **CTRL** (what the controls offer and do), **DRAW** (the marks on the page), **FILTER** (with and without participant data), **STAT** (the statistics line: what R is asked, what R is sent, how its answer is printed, the logarithmic scale and the expected results), **FIT** (the fitted line), **BRUSH** (a region), **LIST** (the listing), **PROF** (the participant profile), **LIFE** (lifecycle, and being opened by another chart), **MOBILE** (a 390-pixel viewport), **SITE** (gallery, demo and API reference) and **LIVE** (the demo against real R in the browser).
