# biomarker-screen requirements matrix

> Requirement matrix for the bio.viz **biomarker screen** (`src/biomarker-screen.js` and `src/biomarker-screen/`, exported as `BioViz.biomarkerScreen`). Written with task [bio.viz#36](https://github.com/jwildfire/bio.viz/issues/36), parent requirement [obot.roadmap#358](https://github.com/jwildfire/obot.roadmap/issues/358); design [353_design.html](https://jwildfire.github.io/obot.roadmap/requirements/design/353_design.html). Every row is drafted by an agent and not yet reviewed by @jwildfire.

## Scope

bio.viz's fourth chart, and the way in to the single charts. One row per biomarker for a comparison chosen once: a standardised difference between two groups, or a correlation with one fixed variable, a participant-level number or a biomarker at a visit. Each row has R's estimate and its interval on one shared axis without units, with R's p-value beside it twice, unadjusted and adjusted across the rows by Benjamini-Hochberg or Holm, and the counts it used. The rows are ordered by R's estimate, by name or by R's adjusted p-value, and paged. A row opens the group comparison or the association scatter for its biomarker, in place, with a way back.

The chart is built from safety.viz's kit, loaded beside bio.viz on the page and never bundled into it, its frame comes from the core, and what it shares with the other three charts is written once, in `src/shared/`.

Every row's estimate, interval, p-value and adjustment is R's: the chart hands R the frame through the connection, once per screen, and draws what gsm.bio's `Analyze_Screen` returns. Each row's p-value is the one the matching single chart's function returns for that biomarker.

Out of scope, and absent on purpose: the computation of any estimate, interval, p-value or adjustment; ordering by any key R did not return; the hazard-ratio rows, which `Analyze_Screen` has and which arrive with stratified survival ([obot.roadmap#360](https://github.com/jwildfire/obot.roadmap/issues/360)). The chart from R, with its statistics stored in the page, is gsm.bio's widget ([gsm.bio#16](https://github.com/jwildfire/gsm.bio/issues/16)).

## Requirement IDs

Prefix **BS**, with these areas: **KIT** (safety.viz's kit, the bundles, the shared parts and the absence of inference), **CFG** (settings), **DATA** (the rows, the frame, the two groups, the order and the shared axis), **LIMIT** (paging), **CTRL** (what the controls offer and do), **DRAW** (the rows on the page), **LIST** (the download), **STAT** (what R is asked, how its answer is printed, the waiting state and the no-stale rule, stored results, and the expected results), **DRILL** (a row opens its chart, and the way back), **FILTER** (filters that let nobody through), **LIFE** (the lifecycle), **MOBILE** (a 390-pixel-wide viewport), **SITE** (the gallery, the demo and the reference), **LIVE** (the demo against real R in the browser).
