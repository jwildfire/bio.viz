# cross-tab requirements matrix

> Requirement matrix for the bio.viz **cross-tabulation** (`src/cross-tab.js` and `src/cross-tab/`, exported as `BioViz.crossTab`). Written with task [bio.viz#44](https://github.com/jwildfire/bio.viz/issues/44), under requirement [obot.roadmap#359](https://github.com/jwildfire/obot.roadmap/issues/359). AI-drafted and not yet reviewed by @jwildfire.

## Scope

bio.viz's fifth chart: is this category associated with that one? A two-way table of counts with its totals and percentages, beside stacked bars of the same numbers, and R's chi-square or Fisher's exact test of it, computed by gsm.bio's `Analyze_Contingency` through the connection to R, one request per table. Either variable is a column or a biomarker cut by the core's shared cut rule. A cell lists its participants and opens the participant profile.

The chart counts, totals and works out percentages, which describe the table. Out of scope, and absent on purpose: the computation of any test statistic, p-value or expected count; the small-expected warning, which is R's; any adjustment.

## Requirement IDs

Prefix **CT**, with these areas: **KIT** (safety.viz's kit), **CFG** (settings), **DATA** (the table), **DRAW** (what is drawn), **STAT** (the statistics line), **LIST** (the listing and profile), **FAIL** and **DROP** (failures and what is left out), **MOBILE** and **SITE** (a phone and the site), **LIVE** (R in the browser).
