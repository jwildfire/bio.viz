# output requirements matrix

> Requirement matrix for getting results out of the browser: what every bio.viz chart does so that what it shows can leave the page and still say what it is. Written with the titles and footnotes ([bio.viz#66](https://github.com/jwildfire/bio.viz/issues/66)), extended with the downloads ([bio.viz#67](https://github.com/jwildfire/bio.viz/issues/67)) and the specification ([bio.viz#68](https://github.com/jwildfire/bio.viz/issues/68)); parent requirement [obot.roadmap#361](https://github.com/jwildfire/obot.roadmap/issues/361). Design: [353_design.html](https://jwildfire.github.io/obot.roadmap/requirements/design/353_design.html), section Getting results out.

## Scope

Every chart's title, subtitle and footnotes: settings of text with named placeholders, filled from the view drawn, as text, with nothing evaluated, and drawn in the chart's own frame above and under what it draws, at every width. One footnote the chart writes last: the date drawn, the bio.viz version, and R's method and counts behind each statistic printed, with the R and gsm.bio versions of a stored result. The rules are written once, in `src/shared/titles.js`, and reached by a page as `BioViz.output`.

Every chart's downloads: a PNG of its frame with the title and footnotes drawn in, at a stated resolution, carrying its title and footnotes in the file; the statistics R returned for the view as CSV; and the table the chart drew from as CSV, every CSV written by RFC 4180.

Every chart's specification: its settings as its controls now read and its filters in force, written as JSON data with a committed JSON schema, and read back by `BioViz.fromSpecification` into the same chart, with nothing in it evaluated and anything that is not a setting, an operator or data refused with a sentence. The interface that collects specifications while someone explores belongs to an app and is not in scope; running them is gsm.bio's batch runner ([obot.roadmap#362](https://github.com/jwildfire/obot.roadmap/issues/362)).
