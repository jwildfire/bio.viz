# group-comparison requirements matrix

> Requirement matrix for the bio.viz **group comparison** chart (`src/group-comparison.js` and `src/group-comparison/`, exported as `BioViz.groupComparison`). Written with task [bio.viz#9](https://github.com/jwildfire/bio.viz/issues/9), parent requirement [obot.roadmap#355](https://github.com/jwildfire/obot.roadmap/issues/355), and extended with task [bio.viz#16](https://github.com/jwildfire/bio.viz/issues/16), parent requirement [obot.roadmap#356](https://github.com/jwildfire/obot.roadmap/issues/356), which prints R's tests on the chart, task [bio.viz#17](https://github.com/jwildfire/bio.viz/issues/17), which opens the chart on every biomarker at every visit, and task [bio.viz#84](https://github.com/jwildfire/bio.viz/issues/84), parent requirement [obot.roadmap#367](https://github.com/jwildfire/obot.roadmap/issues/367), which makes that opening view a trend tile per biomarker and leaves unscheduled visits out, and task [bio.viz#85](https://github.com/jwildfire/bio.viz/issues/85), of the same requirement, which draws one biomarker across its visits with R's test under each; design [353_design.html](https://jwildfire.github.io/obot.roadmap/requirements/design/353_design.html), sections Core chart types (group comparison), The shared core, How a p-value is shown, Data the charts expect, Repositories, and Risks and open points. The chart's interface is written out in [`docs/group-comparison.md`](../docs/group-comparison.md).

## Scope

bio.viz's first chart. One value across the levels of a category at chosen visits, as boxes, violins or points, with the number in each group beneath; a second grouping by colour, panels by one further variable and a logarithmic scale; controls for all of them; filters when there is participant data; a listing of the participants behind a box or a point; safety.viz's participant profile from a row of it; and a statistics line that prints what R answers.

The chart is built from safety.viz's kit, loaded beside bio.viz on the page and never bundled into it, and its rows come from the core's frame.

The statistics line prints a test of the groups that R computed: the chart chooses which test to ask for (a Welch t-test or a Wilcoxon rank-sum test between two groups, a one-way ANOVA or a Kruskal-Wallis test across more, with pairwise comparisons on request), hands R the rows of each panel through the connection, and prints what gsm.bio's `Analyze_GroupDifference` returns. In the browser, R is given gsm.bio's one statistics file, vendored here with a record of its commit, and what it answers on the gallery's demo is held to what desktop R answers for the same rows.

The chart opens on every biomarker: a trend tile for each, with a line per group through the group's median at each visit, on the biomarker's own value axis, which is never narrower than a set multiple of the standard deviation of the results at the baseline visit. The tiles print no test and ask R for nothing, and every biomarker is drawn.

A tile opens its biomarker over time: with a biomarker chosen and every visit it has, the chart draws that biomarker across the visits in one picture, visit along the bottom in visit order and the groups side by side in their colours at each visit, as boxes, as means with standard errors or as medians with quartiles. Under the axis a table gives the number in each group at each visit and R's test of the groups at each visit, asked of R in one request through gsm.bio's `Analyze_GroupDifferenceBy`, with a switch that has R adjust the p-values across the visits. A visit's name opens that visit alone, the single-visit view; some of the visits, not all, are a panel each. A trail above the chart names the level drawn and leads back. Unscheduled visits are left out of the chart at every level unless they are switched on, by safety.viz's rule under safety.viz's setting names.

Out of scope, and absent on purpose: the computation of any statistical test, estimate, interval, p-value or adjustment, Those are R's. A biomarker cut into groups is the core's shared cut rule ([bio.viz#43](https://github.com/jwildfire/bio.viz/issues/43)), held to desktop R in the core's matrix; this chart takes one as its category and its panels. What the chart works out itself is description of the values it draws: counts, the quantiles and mean a box is drawn from, and the outline of a violin. Those are held to desktop R on the synthetic study. The chart from R, with the opening view's statistics stored in the page, is gsm.bio's widget ([gsm.bio#9](https://github.com/jwildfire/gsm.bio/issues/9)).

## Requirement IDs

Prefix **GC**, with these areas: **KIT** (safety.viz's bundle, vendored and loaded beside bio.viz), **CFG** (settings), **BOX** and **VIOLIN** (the numbers a mark is drawn from), **DATA** (panels and cells), **CTRL** (what the controls offer and do), **DRAW** (the marks on the page), **FILTER** (with and without participant data), **LIST** (the listing), **PROF** (the participant profile), **STAT** (the statistics line: which test R is asked for, what R is sent, how its answer is printed, R's vendored statistics file and the expected results), **LIFE** (lifecycle), **MOBILE** (a 390-pixel viewport), **OVW** (the view of every biomarker the chart opens on, and the defaults that open on it), **TILE** (the trend tiles that view is made of: their points, their value axis and their settings), **LVL** (which level is drawn), **TIME** (one biomarker over time: its picture, its table and its row of tests), **UNS** (unscheduled visits) and **SITE** (gallery, demo and API reference).

**Retired IDs.** GC-OVW-003, GC-OVW-004, GC-OVW-005 and GC-OVW-008 are retired with [bio.viz#84](https://github.com/jwildfire/bio.viz/issues/84) and not reused. They described the overview v0.2.0 opened on, a small panel for every visit of every biomarker, drawn a page of biomarkers at a time, which the trend tiles replace: its rows and panels (003) are GC-TILE-001, its panels as the biomarker's own (004) GC-TILE-002, its value axis (005) GC-TILE-004, and its pages (008) are gone, every biomarker being drawn (GC-OVW-015). The other GC-OVW rows are kept under their IDs and rewritten for the tiles, each saying so in its Notes.
