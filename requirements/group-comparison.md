# group-comparison requirements matrix

> Requirement matrix for the bio.viz **group comparison** chart (`src/group-comparison.js` and `src/group-comparison/`, exported as `BioViz.groupComparison`). Written with task [bio.viz#9](https://github.com/jwildfire/bio.viz/issues/9); parent requirement [obot.roadmap#355](https://github.com/jwildfire/obot.roadmap/issues/355), design [353_design.html](https://jwildfire.github.io/obot.roadmap/requirements/design/353_design.html), sections Core chart types (group comparison), The shared core, How a p-value is shown, Data the charts expect, Repositories, and Risks and open points. The chart's interface is written out in [`docs/group-comparison.md`](../docs/group-comparison.md).

## Scope

bio.viz's first chart. One value across the levels of a category at chosen visits, as boxes, violins or points, with the number in each group beneath; a second grouping by colour, panels by one further variable and a logarithmic scale; controls for all of them; filters when there is participant data; a listing of the participants behind a box or a point; safety.viz's participant profile from a row of it; and a statistics line that prints what R answers.

The chart is built from safety.viz's kit, loaded beside bio.viz on the page and never bundled into it, and its rows come from the core's frame.

Out of scope, and absent on purpose: every statistical test, estimate, interval, p-value and adjustment, the controls that choose them, and the rule that cuts a continuous variable into groups. Those are R's, and arrive with the next requirement ([obot.roadmap#356](https://github.com/jwildfire/obot.roadmap/issues/356)). What the chart works out itself is description of the values it draws: counts, the quantiles and mean a box is drawn from, and the outline of a violin. Those are held to desktop R on the synthetic study.

## Requirement IDs

Prefix **GC**, with these areas: **KIT** (safety.viz's bundle, vendored and loaded beside bio.viz), **CFG** (settings), **BOX** and **VIOLIN** (the numbers a mark is drawn from), **DATA** (panels and cells), **CTRL** (what the controls offer and do), **DRAW** (the marks on the page), **FILTER** (with and without participant data), **LIST** (the listing), **PROF** (the participant profile), **STAT** (the statistics line), **LIFE** (lifecycle), **MOBILE** (a 390-pixel viewport) and **SITE** (gallery, demo and API reference).
