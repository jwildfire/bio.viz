# correlation-matrix requirements matrix

> Requirement matrix for the bio.viz **correlation matrix** (`src/correlation-matrix.js` and `src/correlation-matrix/`, exported as `BioViz.correlationMatrix`). Written with task [bio.viz#27](https://github.com/jwildfire/bio.viz/issues/27), parent requirement [obot.roadmap#357](https://github.com/jwildfire/obot.roadmap/issues/357); design [353_design.html](https://jwildfire.github.io/obot.roadmap/requirements/design/353_design.html). Every row is drafted by an agent and not yet reviewed by @jwildfire.

## Scope

bio.viz's third chart, and the overview for the association scatter. A grid over a set of variables, either several biomarkers at one visit or one biomarker at several visits, all with one value type: below the diagonal each pair is a mark sized and coloured by its correlation coefficient, above it the coefficient as a number, and every cell has its own pair count, in its name, under the grid while it is pointed at or focused, and in a list beneath. For six variables or fewer the pairs can be drawn as small scatters. A cell opens the association scatter for its pair, in place, with a way back.

The chart is built from safety.viz's kit, loaded beside bio.viz on the page and never bundled into it, its frame comes from the core, and what it shares with the other two charts is written once, in `src/shared/`.

Every coefficient, interval and pair count is R's: the chart hands R the frame through the connection, once per grid, and draws what gsm.bio's `Analyze_CorrelationMatrix` returns. The minimum number of complete pairs a cell needs is R's too: the chart has a control for it, and what the control holds is handed to R. The grid prints no p-value, by design.

Out of scope, and absent on purpose: the computation of any coefficient, interval, p-value or adjustment; a p-value anywhere on the grid; clustering or ordering the variables by their coefficients. The chart from R, with its statistics stored in the page, is gsm.bio's widget ([gsm.bio#13](https://github.com/jwildfire/gsm.bio/issues/13)).

## Requirement IDs

Prefix **CM**, with these areas: **KIT** (safety.viz's kit, the bundles, the shared parts and the absence of inference), **CFG** (settings), **DATA** (the variables, the frame, the cells and a mark), **LIMIT** (how many variables are drawn), **CTRL** (what the controls offer and do), **DRAW** (the grid on the page), **FILTER** (filters that let nobody through), **LIST** (the list of pairs), **STAT** (what R is asked, how its answer is printed, the waiting state and the no-stale rule, stored results, and the expected results), **DRILL** (a cell opens the scatter, and the way back), **LIFE** (the lifecycle), **MOBILE** (a 390-pixel-wide viewport), **SITE** (the gallery, the demo and the reference), **LIVE** (the demo against real R in the browser).
