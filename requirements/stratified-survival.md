# stratified-survival requirements matrix

> Requirement matrix for the bio.viz **stratified survival chart** (`src/stratified-survival.js` and `src/stratified-survival/`, exported as `BioViz.stratifiedSurvival`). Written with task [bio.viz#61](https://github.com/jwildfire/bio.viz/issues/61), under requirement [obot.roadmap#360](https://github.com/jwildfire/obot.roadmap/issues/360). AI-drafted and not yet reviewed by @jwildfire.

## Scope

bio.viz's sixth chart: do participants with high and low levels of this biomarker have different outcomes? Kaplan–Meier curves per group, from safety.viz's kit (`kmEstimate`), with censor marks and an at-risk strip, above a histogram of the biomarker with its cut line, which can be dragged. Under it R's log-rank test, each group's median survival with its log-log interval and, for two groups, the hazard ratio with its interval, computed by gsm.bio's `Analyze_Survival` through the connection to R, one request per view. The groups are a column or a biomarker cut by the core's shared cut rule. A curve or an at-risk count lists its participants and opens the participant profile.

The chart draws the kit's descriptive estimate and counts who is at risk. Out of scope, and absent on purpose: a confidence band, worked out or drawn; the computation of any test statistic, p-value, median or hazard ratio; any adjustment. The kit's estimator is Experimental until its clinical review.

## Requirement IDs

Prefix **SS**, with these areas: **KIT** (safety.viz's kit), **CFG** (settings), **DATA** (who is drawn), **CURVE** (the curves), **RISK** (the at-risk strip), **CUT**, **HIST** and **DRAG** (the cut line and its histogram), **DRAW** (what is drawn), **STAT** (the statistics line), **NEED** and **EVENT** (the outcomes table), **LIST** (the listing and profile), **FAIL** (failures), **MOBILE** and **SITE** (a phone and the site), **LIVE** (R in the browser).
