Stratified survival fixture

Playwright harness page for the stratified survival chart (#61). It loads safety.viz's vendored bundle and then bio.viz's committed bundle, as two script tags, and draws the chart on the vendored synthetic study. ?data= chooses the tables: all (the results, the participant table and the outcomes table, the default) or no-outcomes (the results and the participant table). The chart opens on event-free survival by CRP at Baseline cut at its median. ?make=no loads the tables and makes no chart, for a test that makes its own. The tests reach the chart as window.__ss.chart.
