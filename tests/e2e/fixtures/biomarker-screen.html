Biomarker screen fixture

Playwright harness page for the biomarker screen (#36). It loads safety.viz's vendored bundle and then bio.viz's committed bundle, as two script tags, and draws the chart on the vendored synthetic study. ?data= chooses the tables: both (the results and the participant table, the default), results (the results table as vendored, alone), outcomes (the results, the participant table and the outcomes table, for a hazard ratio) or many-biomarkers (the derived fixture of thirty-six biomarkers, with the participant table). The chart is made with its defaults, which open on every biomarker at the first visit, a difference between the first column of groups' first two groups, and the one setting the study needs, its baseline visit. ?make=no loads the tables and makes no chart, for a test that makes its own. The tests reach the chart as window.__bs.chart.
