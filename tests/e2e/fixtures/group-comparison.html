Group comparison fixture

Playwright harness page for the group comparison chart (#9). It loads safety.viz's vendored bundle and then bio.viz's committed bundle, as two script tags, and draws the chart on the vendored synthetic study. ?data= chooses the tables: both (the results and the participant table, the default), results (the results table as vendored, alone) or results-with-arm (the derived fixture: results with ARM carried on the rows, and no participant table). The tests reach the chart as window.__gc.chart.
