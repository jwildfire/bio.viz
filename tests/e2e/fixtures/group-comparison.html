Group comparison fixture

Playwright harness page for the group comparison chart (#9). It loads safety.viz's vendored bundle and then bio.viz's committed bundle, as two script tags, and draws the chart on the vendored synthetic study. ?data= chooses the tables: both (the results and the participant table, the default), results (the results table as vendored, alone), results-with-arm (the derived fixture: results with ARM carried on the rows, and no participant table), arm-sex (both tables, with the demo's four-level column ARM_SEX added to the participants) or many-biomarkers (the derived fixture of thirty-six biomarkers, with the participant table). The biomarker and the visit the chart opens on are stated here, IL-6 at Week 4, not left to the chart's defaults; a test of the trend tiles sets both to null, and a test of one biomarker over time sets the visit to null. The tests reach the chart as window.__gc.chart.
