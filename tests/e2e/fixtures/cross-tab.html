Cross-tabulation fixture

Playwright harness page for the cross-tabulation (#44). It loads safety.viz's vendored bundle and then bio.viz's committed bundle, as two script tags, and draws the chart on the vendored synthetic study. ?data= chooses the tables: both (the results and the participant table, the default) or results (the results table alone). The chart opens on arm by response. ?make=no loads the tables and makes no chart, for a test that makes its own. The tests reach the chart as window.__ct.chart.
