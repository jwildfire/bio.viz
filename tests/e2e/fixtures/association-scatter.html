Association scatter fixture

Playwright harness page for the association scatter (#26). It loads safety.viz's vendored bundle and then bio.viz's committed bundle, as two script tags, and draws the chart on the vendored synthetic study. ?data= chooses the tables: both (the results and the participant table, the default), results (the results table as vendored, alone) or results-with-arm (the derived fixture: results with ARM carried on the rows, and no participant table). The pair the chart opens on is stated here, TNF-alpha against IL-10 at Baseline, not left to the chart's defaults; a test of the defaults sets x and y to null. ?make=no loads the tables and makes no chart, for a test that makes its own. The tests reach the chart as window.__as.chart.
